# Executive Summary

*Data covers September 2016 to October 2018. The first few months of 2016 and the last two months of 2018 have very few orders because the dataset starts and ends partway through, so they're left out of the trend analysis.*


### Business Health

The business is healthy. Out of 99,441 orders, 97% were delivered (96,478) and only 0.63% were canceled (625). Orders grew steadily through 2017, from 800 in January to a peak of 7,544 in November (likely boosted by Black Friday). Through 2018, volume held strong at around 6,750 orders per month from January to August, which shows the growth from 2017 stuck.


### Top Categories

Review scores are consistent across the top categories, all between 3.9 and 4.15 stars, so revenue and volume are the better guides for where to invest. I'd double down on health/beauty and bed/bath/table. They're both in the top 3 for revenue ($1.43M and $1.24M) and are the top 2 categories by items sold. I'd also double down on watches/gifts. It's second in revenue ($1.30M) with 38% fewer items sold than health/beauty, meaning each sale brings in more money ($218 per item vs. $149). That could make it more profitable, though I'd need cost data to prove it.


### Customer Geography & Delivery

Our customers are heavily concentrated in São Paulo (SP), which accounts for about 42% of all customers (41,746). Together with Rio de Janeiro (RJ) and Minas Gerais (MG), the top 3 states make up two-thirds of our customer base. Delivery takes 12.1 days on average, and delivery speed lines up closely with how customers rate us. Orders with 5-star reviews arrived in 10.2 days on average, while 1-star orders took 20.8 days, twice as long.


### Recommendation

Prioritize delivery speed, with a goal of getting the average delivery time under 10 days, which is where 5-star reviews are. Faster delivery isn't guaranteed to raise reviews on its own, but the pattern is strong enough to be worth testing. The goal would be to raise our company-wide review average from 4.2 (across 96,359 reviews) to above 4.5.
